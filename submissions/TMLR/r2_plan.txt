OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Three rules govern the ordering below. First, several expensive experiments have a cheap first step that decides whether the expensive part is needed at all; those cheap steps are pulled into Phase 0 and are called gates. Second, the manuscript restructure runs in two passes, because the final choice of which judge is primary cannot be justified until the human gold set exists; Pass 1 builds the paired structure, Pass 2 fixes the wording once the gold set lands. Third, guardrails from r1 remain binding: round-trip validate any reused wrapper against the recorded CSV, reweight stratified samples to base rate before any marginal claim, never drop rows (empty, malformed and error rows all carry a status field), never tune decoding to improve a result, update the narrative when a control contradicts it, fixed generation seeds, judge temperature fixed to 0, Wilson intervals beside every proportion, JSON written with indent=4.

DEFINITION OF DONE
================================================================================

1. The abstract states the primary finding as judge-conditional, and every headline number is paired (official, common) or a primary judge is declared with a justification grounded in the gold set.
2. A third-provider judge and a blinded two-annotator gold set exist, Cohen's kappa is reported, per-judge precision and recall against gold are reported and reweighted to base rate, and the "two independent judges" wording is gone.
3. Falcon ASR is reported per cell both unconditionally and conditional on a final answer, and Dream, DiffuCoder and LLaDA have matching capability controls at n of roughly 200.
4. RQ2 is answered by the extended mixed model with McNemar paired comparisons; FSR is gone or footnoted with its leave-one-out range; cosine and ACTR are removed.
5. The six diffusion cells have three-seed replication at roughly 300 prompts per cell with reported seed variance; judge temperature is fixed to 0 and stated; reported precision matches the uncertainty.
6. The ArrAttack protocol contradiction is resolved in writing, and the full-pool run exists if the pipeline is transfer-only.
7. RQ3 is answered with a model or withdrawn; Figure 3 is exploratory with a corrected axis and a prominent note on the "Other policy" residual.
8. Every table and text inconsistency listed in Step 2 is reconciled and verifiable from r2_num_reconcile.md.
9. Decode accuracy sits beside MetaCipher ASR, the DeepSeek circularity is named, and no gate in the four-gate framework is described as tested without a control.
10. The reproducibility package exists: anonymized code and verdicts, pinned hashes, verbatim judge prompts, judge sampling parameters, total compute, disclosure statement, and a specified access policy for restricted fields.
