The insert cipher encodes a word by inserting one random letter to its second place and another random letter to its second-last place. In such a way, the original word is undistinguishable. To decrypt it, remove the letters from the second and second-last places. For example, the cipher "rleadeer" is decrypted to "reader"; the cipher "cromputeer" is decrypted to "computer"; the cipher "hyelluo" is decrypted to "hello". The letters inserted are random and can be any letter from the alphabet. The original word is a common English word, but the ciphered word is not guaranteed to be a valid English word.
