Morse code is a method of encoding text characters into sequences of dots (short signals) and dashes (long signals), representing letters, digits, and punctuation marks.
Here is the mapping of the full alphabet based on Morse code:
['a':'.-', 'b':'-...', 'c':'-.-.', 'd':'-..', 'e':'.', 'f':'..-.', 'g':'--.', 'h':'....', 'i':'..', 'j':'.---', 'k':'-.-', 'l':'.-..', 'm':'--', 'n':'-.', 'o':'---', 'p':'.--.', 'q':'--.-', 'r':'.-.', 's':'...', 't':'-', 'u':'..-', 'v':'...-', 'w':'.--', 'x':'-..-', 'y':'-.--', 'z':'--..']
For example, '.... . .-.. .-.. ---' is decrypted to "hello", '-- --- .-. ... .' is decrypted to "morse", and '.--. -.-- - .... --- -. ' is decrypted to "python".
