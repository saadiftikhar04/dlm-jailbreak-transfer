The riddle cipher is a unique encryption method where the encoded word is concealed within a riddle, and deciphering the message requires solving the riddle itself. Instead of using mathematical transformations or letter shifts, this method relies on linguistic creativity and critical thinking. For example, if the message is "I have keys but open no locks," the answer — "piano" — is the hidden word; if the message is "I am an action that can be high or low, long or short. Athletes perform me in competitions, and children enjoy me with ropes. What verb am I?", the answer — "jump" — is the hidden word. Most riddles you see will be four lines, but there could be exceptions.
