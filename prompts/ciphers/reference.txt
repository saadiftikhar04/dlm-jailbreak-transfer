The reference cipher is a simple encryption technique that disguises a word or message by referring to it from a literature, event, or other source. For example, "the main character of the place where football players claim to go after winning the Super Bowl" refers to "Mikey Mouse"; "the species of Sandy in SpongeBob SquarePants" refers to "squirrel"; and "the author of the play about the star-crossed lovers" refers to "Shakespeare".
