Anagram is a simple encryption technique that disguises a message by rearranging the letters of each word or phrase into a different order. To decrypt the message, the recipient must rearrange the letters back into the original order. For example, if the encrypted word is 'lehlo', and the 3rd and 1st characters are swapped, the original word is 'hello'; if the encrypted word is 'psruue', and the 2nd and the 4th characters are swapped, the original word is 'pursue'.
