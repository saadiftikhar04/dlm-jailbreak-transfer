Pig Latin is a playful form of coded language often used by English speakers for amusement. To decode it, you reverse the transformation: if a word ends in "way," it likely started with a vowel, so you simply remove "way" to get the original word. If a word ends in "ay" and has a consonant or consonant cluster before it, you move that part to the beginning. 
For example, the Pig Latin word 'ellohay' is decrypted to 'hello', and 'appleway' is decrypted to 'apple'. Similarly, 'endsfray' becomes 'friends', 'oolschay' becomes 'school', and 'unningray' becomes 'running'.
