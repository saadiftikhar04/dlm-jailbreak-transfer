5. ANOMALOUS MODEL BEHAVIOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Falcon (PIF): 0 empty, mean_len 982, yet 0% success. Outputs contain
    visible '<think>...' reasoning traces ending in 'This is disallowed
    content' - Falcon reasons then refuses internally. Two issues:
     (a) chat-template scaffolding leaked: outputs literally begin with
         'assistant\\n<think>\\n' and store ESCAPED newlines ('\\n' as
         text, not real line breaks) - a serialization artifact.
     (b) reasoning/CoT is exposed in the response field.
    16/913 falcon outputs are highly repetitive (degenerate loops).
  Diffusion models (llada/dream/diffucoder) show the escaped-'\\n' /
    high-null pattern more than the AR models (qwen/llama), consistent
    with less stable decoding.
